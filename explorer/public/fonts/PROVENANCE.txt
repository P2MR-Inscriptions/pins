Family name in the font: Chakra Petch.
Google Fonts records the designer as Cadson Demak.
The upstream README also names Theerawat Pojvibulsiri as the designer of the face.
The name table credits Katatrad Aksorn Co.,Ltd.
Copyright 2018 The Chakra Petch Project Authors (https://github.com/m4rc1e/Chakra-Petch.git).

Source files, unmodified before subsetting:
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/chakrapetch
  ChakraPetch-Regular.ttf (weight 400)
  ChakraPetch-Medium.ttf (weight 500)
  ChakraPetch-Bold.ttf (weight 700)
Google Fonts upstream_info.md says that tree tracks https://github.com/m4rc1e/Chakra-Petch,
which is the working fork of the original https://github.com/cadsondemak/Chakra-Petch.

License: SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full text is OFL.txt in this directory.

These woff2 files are a Latin subset of those three weights, made locally with fontTools.
The subset keeps ASCII and the rest of Latin-1, plus the usual Latin punctuation range.
No other weights are shipped. The page loads these files from this origin only.
